# A Discord ticket bot for player reports, appeals and support

Give players one place to ask for help, and give the right staff a private channel to handle it. HAF Bot creates Discord Tickets with intake questions, staff claiming and transcripts.

**Start free with one panel and one Ticket type.** Add more types or advanced automation only when your community needs them.

[Add HAF Bot to Discord](https://hafbot.xyz/install) · [Compare all plan limits](/plans)

## Start with one support queue

You do not need separate panels for every possible problem. On Free, use one type such as **Player support**, and ask the member what they need help with.

A suggested intake for a gaming community is:

- Which game server is this about?
- What happened, and approximately when?
- Which player or account is involved?

Ask only for information staff need to resolve the request. Never ask for passwords, account tokens or payment-card details.

The member selects the Ticket type and answers the configured questions. HAF creates a private Discord channel for the member, the selected support role and the bot. Staff can claim the Ticket so responsibility is clear.

## Get a working panel into Discord

1. [Install HAF](https://hafbot.xyz/install), then open the [dashboard](https://hafbot.xyz/admin) and select the correct Discord server.
2. Open **Tickets**. On **Panel**, choose or create the panel channel, private Ticket category and transcript channel.
3. On **Types**, add **Player support**, choose the staff support role and add the intake questions you need.
4. Select **Save Changes**, then **Publish Panel**. Saving alone does not publish the member-facing message.
5. Resolve **Setup Check** warnings. Test with a non-staff account: open a Ticket, check who can see it, send a reply and close it.

Before announcing the panel, confirm that ordinary members cannot see another member's Ticket or the staff transcript channel. A passing setup check is not a substitute for this real-member test.

## Staff anonymity is different from an anonymous report

On paid plans, anonymous support lets authorised staff write replies in a private staff thread and relay them through HAF Bot. The member sees the bot reply rather than the individual moderator's identity.

This does **not** make the member anonymous, and authorised staff records retain the real staff actor. See [Anonymous Support](/features/tickets#anonymous-support) before enabling it.

## Moving from another ticket bot

Pilot HAF with a staff test channel before replacing your existing support panel. Keep existing Tickets and transcripts in the old system until you have finished handling them. This guide does not provide a ticket-history importer.

HAF saves the transcript before deleting a successfully closed Ticket channel. Closed Tickets cannot currently be reopened; continued discussion needs a new Ticket. New HTML links have a 30-day access period; managed archives have a 365-day retention period followed by scheduled cleanup. Discord copies are separate. The dashboard has no self-service retention setting or transcript deletion, so review the [transcript limitations](/features/tickets#transcripts-and-closed-tickets) before collecting sensitive information.

A Discord Ticket panel also requires the member to have access to your Discord server. It is not an off-server appeal form for someone banned from Discord itself.
